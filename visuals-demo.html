Argentex (Pty) Ltd

Custom visuals for review

Five bespoke, hand-built motifs to replace the stock photography. The brief is sober and editorial, so these are fine-line illustrations in the brand palette, the way a serious report or a banknote is engraved, rather than glossy graphics. Motion is slow and switches off for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

Nothing here is on the live site yet. Tell me which to use and where, and I will place them.

Motif 01 · Sun

Rising sun

An engraved sun on the horizon, rays turning almost imperceptibly. Reads as energy and renewables without a single gradient. Natural hero backdrop or section opener.

Motif 02 · Silver

Recovery loop

The business model as a quiet line diagram: panel, recovery, bullion, economy, with value circling back home. A travelling oxblood arc keeps it alive. Pairs with section III.

Motif 03 · Silver

Refined bullion

A single cast bar, stamped Ag .9999, with a slow specular sweep, the one place a metallic sheen is allowed. Sits well on the dark positioning block.

Motif 04 · Sun and nature

Solar field at the horizon

Line-art panels under a low sun, with a soft sheen passing across the array. Sun, renewables and the South African landscape in one quiet frame. Good for the hero.

Motif 05 · Nature

Contour drift

Topographic lines drifting slowly, like a survey map of the land. A calm, full-width band to separate sections or sit behind the footer. The quietest of the set.
